# Nimma-Guru 📚✨

### Community Mentorship Android Application

Nimma-Guru is an Android application developed to connect retired professionals, teachers, engineers, and skilled mentors (“Gurus”) with students who need educational support in their local communities. The platform encourages knowledge sharing and community learning through a simple and user-friendly interface.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Many retired professionals in villages and local communities wish to contribute to society by teaching and mentoring students. However, there is no proper platform to connect these voluntary mentors with students who require after-school academic guidance.

Nimma-Guru solves this problem by creating a digital mentorship platform where students can easily find nearby mentors based on skills and availability.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

The vision of Nimma-Guru is to build a community-driven educational ecosystem where experienced individuals can share their knowledge and help students improve their learning outside the classroom.

The application focuses on:

* Community mentorship
* Skill-based learning
* Educational support for students
* Knowledge sharing among generations

---

## 🚀 Features

* 👨‍🏫 Guru Profile Management
  Mentors can create and manage profiles with their skills, subjects, and available timings.

* 🔍 Smart Search & Filter
  Students can search mentors based on subjects like Math, Science, English, Carpentry, etc.

* 📅 Class Calendar
  View upcoming mentorship sessions and local community classes.

* 💬 Appreciation System
  Students can post thank-you notes and appreciation messages for mentors.

* 🌐 Multi-language Support
  Supports both Kannada and English languages.

* ☁️ Real-time Database
  Uses Firebase Firestore for real-time mentor data management.

## 📱 App Workflow

1. Mentor registers and creates a Guru profile.
2. Mentor adds skills, subjects, and free timings.
3. Students search for mentors nearby.
4. Students view available sessions and class schedules.
5. Students connect with mentors and attend sessions.
6. Appreciation notes can be shared after sessions.
